# Support — MOOP

MOOP is a personal movie and series library for Mac, iPhone and Apple Watch. Video playback takes place in your chosen external service.

## Getting started

Add your first title manually or import your library. Random selection, the daily pick and moods use your available titles. Organise favourites, lists, tags and genres, record watched status and its date, add a personal rating and manually track your progress through a series.

The library offers search, sorting, list or poster views and multiple selection. Trash lets you restore removed titles. Metadata searches show information for you to check before saving; accuracy depends on the provider.

Choose an illustration from Photos, a file or an image copied to the clipboard. You can open an image search in the browser, then import an image you are entitled to use. Pasting an image’s web address and extracting HTML pages are not offered in the public version.

## Backups and transfers

JSON export preserves the library and its history, including personal lists and illustrations. CSV transfers title information. IMDb list importing is also available. Keep a backup before an important operation. Recovery copies can help restore an earlier state. Resetting preferences preserves the library.

Mac and iPhone exchange libraries using JSON files; there is no built-in iCloud synchronisation. Apple Watch receives data through Apple mechanisms and needs a paired iPhone for transfers.

## Searches and platforms

The public version starts without a preloaded third-party catalogue. External searches and schedules need an internet connection; results may be incomplete or change. Links to Prime Video, Apple TV, france.tv, ARTE and other platforms open those services. They do not connect your accounts or guarantee availability or inclusion in your subscription.

## Contact us

Email support@recordpicker.app with the MOOP version and build number, your device, its operating system version and steps to reproduce the issue. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords or a complete private library.
