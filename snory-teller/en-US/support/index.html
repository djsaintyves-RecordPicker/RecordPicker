Support

Contact: support@recordpicker.app. Put “Snory Teller” in the subject to distinguish your request from Record Picker support. No account is required.

Prepare a trial

Snory Teller requires an iPhone running iOS 16 or later. Vibrations require a paired Apple Watch running watchOS 9 or later with the app installed. Supported languages are French (France) and English (US).

Place your iPhone near the bed, plug it in, and inform your partner that the microphone will be used. Open Snory Teller on your watch, leave vibrations enabled on both devices and tap “Try a vibration” on your iPhone. Acceptance does not prove that you felt it. Wait five seconds between manual trials.

Tap “Start my night” and allow the microphone. You can lock your iPhone while listening. In the morning, end the session and open “My nights”. Audio interruptions may stop a session; check the displayed message before restarting.

The watch does not vibrate

Bring the devices closer, check pairing, installation and both vibration settings, then open the watch app. The prototype requires the watch app to be active. watchOS may suspend it: overnight vibrations are not guaranteed. Requests expire quickly and are never replayed later. The minimum interval for automatic interventions is two minutes and can increase during a session. Requests are capped at twenty per session.

No Health data appears

Optional access is available in a completed night report. Check that a source recorded sleep during that interval and has finished syncing. Select a source if several appear. An empty result may also mean access was declined; the app cannot distinguish these causes. Snory Teller does not write to Health.

Delete your data

Stop the session, then use “Delete nights” in “My nights”. This does not erase Health data or preferences. Fully deleting the app removes its local storage; offloading may retain it.

Report a problem

Include your iPhone and Watch models, iOS/watchOS versions, Snory Teller version, steps and displayed message. Do not send Health data or audio recordings; hide personal information in any image you attach.

Limitations

Snory Teller is a wellness experience in development. Sound estimates do not measure sleep stages, distinguish your snoring from your partner’s, or provide diagnosis or treatment. No reduction in snoring or partner awakenings is guaranteed.
