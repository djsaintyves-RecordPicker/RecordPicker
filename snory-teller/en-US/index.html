Snory Teller

Better nights. For both of you.

An iPhone and Apple Watch experience for observing nighttime sounds, trying a discreet vibration and reviewing a local report.

In development

The prototype analyzes sound on your iPhone without retaining audio. Vibrations require the watch app to be active; overnight availability is not guaranteed. No sleep apnea diagnosis or treatment, and no promise of snoring reduction.

Your night, on your devices

Deletable local history, optional read-only Health context, no account or advertising. Requires iOS 17 and watchOS 10 or later, with French and US English support.

Detect · Nudge · Learn

Detect an episode, request an intervention, observe what follows and present the results: a loop still requiring real-world validation.
