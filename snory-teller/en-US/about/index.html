About Snory Teller

Better nights. For both of you.

Snory Teller began with a simple situation: we do not always hear ourselves snore, while the person beside us wakes up and asks us to turn over. The aim is to explore a discreet wrist cue that could help preserve both partners’ rest, without guaranteeing that outcome.

Detect · Nudge · Learn

Detect analyzes sounds on your iPhone. Nudge can request a gentle Apple Watch vibration after an episode is confirmed. Learn presents observations and what follows an intervention. The “detect → intervene → verify → learn” loop is central to the project.

The current version is a prototype: the watch app must be active, detection still needs real-world validation and identifying a partner’s snoring remains unresolved. The app provides no sleep apnea diagnosis or treatment.

Creation and credits

Design and development: Yves Durand, with assistance from AI tools. Visual identity: abstract curves in shades of blue selected by Yves Durand, with AI-assisted artwork adaptation. The decorative Watch waveform is a synthetic illustration, not a recording or live measurement.

The app uses Apple frameworks SwiftUI, AVFoundation, SoundAnalysis, WatchConnectivity, WatchKit, HealthKit and Charts. This version integrates no third-party SDK. Apple trademarks and products belong to Apple Inc. Snory Teller is an independent project.
