IPHONE + APPLE WATCH

Understand the sounds of your night.

Snory Teller aims to help interrupt snoring: discreet wrist vibrations prompt the snorer to turn onto their side. Vibration requests repeat while detected snoring continues, with the aim of disturbing their partner less.

The app does not verify your position. It cannot guarantee that snoring will stop or that you or your partner will remain asleep.

Your questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

A watch is required for wrist alerts. Observations and reports are available on your iPhone.

Can the iPhone vibrate too?

Yes. Silent night alarms may also vibrate your iPhone.

Which devices are supported?

iOS 16 or later. Silent night alarms require iOS 26 or later and the relevant permissions. Interface languages: English, French, German and Japanese.

What if two people share the bed?

The app cannot identify who is snoring. Phone placement and surrounding sounds may affect observations.

Why were there no alerts during an analysed night?

Analysed time measures how long the app could listen, not the proportion of snoring it recognised. An alert requires a confirmed episode; brief or scattered sounds may appear in the report without triggering an alert. Check phone placement and test the input gain setting in your bedroom conditions.

Can I make vibrations easier to notice?

On supported devices, night alerts use a system alarm. The app cannot directly adjust the strength of the watch’s haptic motor. In Apple Watch settings, check Sounds & Haptics → Haptics → Prominent, then test while awake with your iPhone locked and your watch on your wrist. Feeling a vibration during this test does not guarantee you will notice it while asleep.

How do I cancel?

Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends. Saved reports remain accessible after expiry.

Alerts depend on your devices and settings. An alert request does not confirm a felt vibration. Snory Teller does not guarantee reduced snoring or improved sleep and does not diagnose or treat sleep apnoea.
