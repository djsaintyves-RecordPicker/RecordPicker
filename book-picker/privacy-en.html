Book Picker privacy policy

Book Picker is a book organization and reading selection app published by Yves Durand. It works without a Book Picker account, advertising, or in-app purchases.

Your library

Books, imported fields, favorites, reading status and pages, notes, loans, tags, lists, custom covers, and draw history are kept in app storage on your device. Book Picker has no server that receives your library and does not include advertising tracking or usage analytics.

Online searches and covers

When you request an ISBN lookup, the ISBN is sent to Open Library to retrieve edition information. Remote covers are loaded from the host recorded in the book details; you can disable remote cover loading in settings. Image search opens Google with the available title, author, and ISBN. Loading an image URL that you enter contacts that image host.

These external services receive the information needed for the request and technical connection information, such as your IP address. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies. Book Picker does not send your entire library for these searches. You can add book details manually without an online lookup.

Camera, photos, and clipboard

The ISBN barcode scanner uses the camera on compatible devices after you choose to scan and grant system permission. Book Picker does not send scanner images to a lookup service; only an ISBN can subsequently be used for a lookup that you request. Manual entry remains available if you decline camera access.

You choose images using system selection tools, files, Continuity Camera on Mac when available, or an explicit paste action. Imported images are resized and re-encoded; original EXIF and location metadata is not retained in the normalized cover.

Apple Watch and devices

The Apple Watch companion receives iPhone library data through WatchConnectivity. Favorite and reading status changes on the watch are transferred to the iPhone. Mac and iPhone libraries remain separate: Book Picker does not provide automatic iCloud sync between them.

Exports and backups

A JSON export can contain your entire library, including personal notes and loan details, covers, history, lists, and some preferences. You choose where to save or share it through system tools. If you use third-party storage or a sharing service, that service’s rules apply to the exported copy. Device backups depend on your system settings.

Retention and deletion

Information remains in app storage. Trash and removed tags are recoverable and are not permanent deletion. Use your operating system’s storage management tools to delete local app data. Exports and other copies must be managed separately at their saved locations.

BookBuddy

BookBuddy is a separate app. Book Picker imports files you select and does not access your BookBuddy account.

Privacy questions and requests

For questions about your data or this policy, contact Yves Durand at support@recordpicker.app. If you email us, your address and the information you choose to send are used to handle your request. Please avoid sending your entire library if a description is sufficient.
