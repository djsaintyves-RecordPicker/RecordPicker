Rediscover your books.

  • Add books by scan, ISBN, manual entry or CSV import.
  • Browse your library as a list, small or large covers.

A library that feels like yours.

Add a book by ISBN scan, ISBN lookup, manual entry or BookBuddy CSV import. Browse a list, small covers or large covers, and sort by title, author surname or date added.

Choose your next read.

Keep favorites and reading lists, narrow your selection by reading status, genre or tag, then make a random pick. Book details bring together your information, notes and custom covers.

Getting started

  1. Add your first book or import your library.
  2. Choose your preferred layout and filters.
  3. Find a read or let a random pick surprise you.
