A companion app for Discogs

Keep Discogs for its release database and marketplace, and use Record Picker to browse, enrich and actually play your collection, in a private catalogue.

Free for up to 100 records · No subscription · No account

What Record Picker gives you

  • Import the collection you already built on Discogs
  • Add personal notes and listening history
  • Choose what to play, with no marketplace or feed

How to start

  1. Export your collection or wantlist as CSV from Discogs.
  2. Import it into Record Picker and check the preview.
  3. With Pro, prepare buying criteria and Discogs alerts from your wishlist.
