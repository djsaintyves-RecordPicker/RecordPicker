How to start

  1. Import your Discogs CSV or add a record manually.
  2. Check editions, formats and duplicates before confirming the import.
  3. Find an album by artist, title or format, then pick your next listen.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for up to 100 records. Beyond that, Light or Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription and no account to create.

Can I mix CDs and vinyl?

Yes. Formats stay distinct in the same collection, and different editions of an album can be kept separately.
