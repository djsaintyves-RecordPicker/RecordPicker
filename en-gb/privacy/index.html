Privacy

Privacy policy

Record Picker is designed to manage a physical music collection without creating a profile of its owner. It has no advertising, no cross-app tracking and no developer-operated user-account system. This policy explains what remains on your devices, what can be synchronised through Apple, and when a limited Internet request is made.

Collection and wishlist data

Records you own or want, favourites, exclusions, tags, notes, ratings, listening and draw history, release details, custom artwork and related metadata are stored by the app on your device.

When iCloud is enabled and available, this data can be synchronised between your devices using your private CloudKit database. Apple operates iCloud and processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy. Record Picker does not copy your collection into a developer-operated database and the developer does not receive a browsable copy of your CloudKit collection.

Badges and achievements

Badges are calculated locally from your owned collection, favourites and saved confirmed listens. They introduce no account, analytics or additional network requests. The existing optional backup and synchronisation paths carry the underlying collection and listening evidence; there is no separate developer-operated badge profile.

Optional cross-platform synchronisation

If you enable cross-platform synchronisation, you select a folder made available by Files or Finder, for example through OneDrive, for use by your Apple, Windows or Android devices. Record Picker does not require or operate a user account for this feature. Collection snapshots, incremental changes and artwork are encrypted and authenticated on the device before they are written to that folder. The association key is kept in the secure credential store of each associated device and is not written separately to the shared folder.

The selected folder provider processes the encrypted files under its own terms and may receive ordinary account, file-transfer and network metadata. Record Picker's developer does not receive the folder, its encryption key or a browsable copy of the collection. The app retains on each device the folder permission, library identifier, device identifier and synchronisation receipts needed to resume safely, reject files from another library and avoid applying a change twice.

You can disconnect one device without deleting the shared folder or affecting other associated devices. Losing the association key on every device makes the encrypted folder unreadable. The separate .recordpicker backup and transfer format remains available independently of synchronisation.

Wishlist buying preferences

Maximum price, currency, priority, desired condition grades and your declaration of configuring an external Discogs alert are personal record data. They remain on the device and are included in record backups and portable exchanges. They follow the existing optional private collection synchronisation paths; they are not sent to a developer-operated service.

Opening Discogs sends only the exact release-page address to your browser. Record Picker does not send the prepared budget or desired grades in that address, collect marketplace prices, read your Discogs account or emails, or activate, verify or delete external alerts. Discogs manages Wantlister and its notifications under its own terms and privacy policy. This feature schedules no local reminders.

Today Pick and music news

Today Pick downloads a small, size-limited public feed of traceable music events from recordpicker.app. The feed is collection-independent: Record Picker does not send your album titles, artists, wishlist, favourites, listening history or other collection data to obtain it. Matching the public events to your collection or wishlist is performed on your device.

If you enable concert suggestions, you choose the location privacy level. A request may contain no region, a two-letter country code, or a deliberately coarse regional code of at most four characters. Your human-readable city name is retained and used locally and is not included in the feed request. Ordinary network information, including your IP address, is necessarily visible to the hosting provider when the public feed is downloaded.

Your actions such as Relevant, Not relevant, More from this source and Less from this source teach Today Pick only on the current device. The local history contains internal record identifiers, public event identifiers, public source names, preference values and dates; it does not contain album titles, artist names, collection metadata or your location. It is size-bounded, older entries expire, it is not synchronised through CloudKit, it is excluded from device backups where the operating system supports that protection, and it can be reset in Today Pick settings.

If you choose “Suggest another source”, Record Picker opens a pre-addressed email in your mail app. Nothing is sent automatically. The developer receives only the content and normal email metadata that you decide to send.

Camera and barcode scanning

Record Picker uses the camera when you explicitly scan a barcode or take a cover photo. Barcode frames are processed locally and are not stored or uploaded by Record Picker; a metadata lookup sends the decoded barcode. A selected cover photo may be saved as artwork in the record and included in collection synchronisation and backups. Printed title and artist extraction runs locally using Vision and, on supported devices, an Apple on-device model. The photo is not uploaded for that extraction; a later metadata search uses only the title or artist required for the requested lookup.

Metadata and artwork searches

When you start a metadata, barcode or artwork lookup, Record Picker may contact services such as MusicBrainz, Discogs, Cover Art Archive, Apple's public catalogue services or Deezer. A request can include the artist, album, catalogue number, barcode or Discogs Release ID needed for the search. Artwork results are labelled with their source and filtered locally before they are shown. If you explicitly choose the separate Internet option, Record Picker opens a DuckDuckGo image search containing only the artist, album title and the words “album cover”; it never sends your collection or wishlist. Those services receive the technical information normally associated with an Internet request, such as your IP address, and apply their own privacy policies and terms.

Local review vocabulary

Reviews retain their original texts and known language/source information in record data. Keyword analysis runs on your device; it does not send review text to a translation service. Personal dictionary additions, labels and exclusions are local preferences. The initial vocabulary includes musical terms only, not the reviews or private records used to establish it.

Critical-review enrichment

When you ask Record Picker to fetch a review for one album or a selected group, it may contact Apple Music, Last.fm, Wikipedia, TheAudioDB, Genius or The Guardian. The lookup includes the artist and album needed to find the text. Fetched text is stored with the corresponding album in your collection and can therefore be included in private CloudKit synchronisation and backups.

Keyword analysis runs on your device. Original reviews are not sent to an external translation service. If you export your keyword preferences, the JSON file contains custom terms, their labels and hidden concepts, but no reviews, collection records or credentials. This file is readable and is shared only with destinations you explicitly choose. Importing shows a preview and preserves existing labels and hidden concepts.

Apple Music access uses MusicKit and requires your authorisation. Last.fm, Genius and The Guardian are contacted only when the corresponding source is enabled and the required personal API credential has been supplied. Credentials are stored in the Apple Keychain for this device and are not sent to Record Picker. Discogs is used for metadata, not as a critical-review source.

“Search a review” opens a search page that you or the app configured in your browser, with the artist and album inserted into the address. Record Picker does not read the resulting web page. The destination website's privacy policy applies.

On-device suggestions and Apple Intelligence

Mood Pick and other recommendation features analyse collection metadata locally. When supported, Record Picker may use Apple's on-device models; otherwise it uses a deterministic local calculation. The developer does not receive the prompt, collection or result.

Collection Graph

On Mac and iPad, Collection Graph derives relationships between documented works, composers, performers, conductors, ensembles, recordings and physical editions from metadata already stored in your collection. Building, searching and comparing this graph takes place on the device. The graph is read-only, does not upload collection relationships to a Record Picker server and does not merge ambiguous records automatically.

Pressing Passports

A Pressing Passport stores the selected Discogs release details and the matrix/runout, catalogue number and barcode observations, private multiline notes and photos that you enter for your own copy. The passport and its optional link to a collection record are stored in the app's private local files. The link uses local library and record identifiers. These files are not included in collection backups, private CloudKit synchronisation or encrypted folder synchronisation.

Loading a Discogs edition sends its Release ID to Discogs; your private inscriptions, notes, photos and record links are not sent with this lookup. Comparisons with the returned evidence happen on your device. Opening saved passports, linking, unlinking and previewing imported passport JSON do not require a network lookup.

Export is a separate, voluntary action and produces readable JSON containing the private inscriptions, notes, photos and source edition details, without the collection link. It is not encrypted by Record Picker. A destination or sharing service you choose handles that copy under its own policies. Protect exported copies and delete them separately when no longer needed. You can delete individual saved passports in the app; unlinking removes only their local association. Keep a separate passport export before removing the app if you want to retain it.

Selected passport photos are resized and re-encoded without EXIF, GPS or comments; their source images are unchanged. Up to eight private photos can be saved in one passport, viewed and removed. Saved photos are included only in a voluntary passport export, not collection synchronisation.

Notifications

If you enable a Today Pick reminder, Record Picker schedules a local notification through Apple's notification system. The developer does not receive your notification schedule. You can disable notifications in the app or in system settings.

Apple Watch

The Apple Watch companion exchanges the information required to display the collection and perform draws with the paired iPhone using Apple's device communication services. This information is not sent to a Record Picker collection server.

Purchases

The optional Record Picker Light and Record Picker Pro lifetime purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, and by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store on Windows. Record Picker receives only the verified entitlement state required to unlock Light or Pro; it does not receive or store payment-card details. Apple's or Microsoft's purchase and privacy terms apply.

Backups, imports and exports

Import, export, backup and restore files are selected and controlled by you. On macOS, Record Picker also creates a verified complete backup when the app quits normally. It stores the current and previous generations in the app's private iCloud Drive folder by default, or in the app's local Documents folder if iCloud is unavailable. These files are not sent to the developer. If you save or share a backup with Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email or another service, that service's privacy practices apply. A complete backup may contain collection data and custom artwork; unlike the optional cross-platform synchronisation folder, an exported backup is not necessarily encrypted, so protect it accordingly.

Diagnostics, analytics and advertising

Record Picker contains no third-party advertising SDK, tracking SDK or developer-run behavioural analytics. Apple may provide the developer with aggregated App Store statistics and, only according to your Apple device sharing choices, diagnostic information. Those reports are governed by Apple's privacy settings and do not give the developer access to your collection.

Contextual and adaptive help keeps only technical tutorial flags and bounded counters on your device, such as whether an explanation was seen, deferred or dismissed. It does not store record titles, artists, moods or listening choices, and this help state is not uploaded to Record Picker, third-party analytics or CloudKit.

Retention, deletion and portability

You can edit or delete collection entries, clear local Today Pick feedback, export open CSV or JSON files, create a complete backup and disconnect a device from a cross-platform library. Deleting the app removes its local data subject to the operating system's backup behaviour, but does not itself delete files in a user-selected synchronisation folder. Data synchronised through iCloud is managed through your Apple account and devices. Before deleting the app, disconnecting every associated device or disabling iCloud, create and verify a full backup if you want to preserve the collection.

The recordpicker.app website

The website measures its audience with Umami Cloud (hosted in Europe), without advertising cookies and without full page addresses. Nothing from your apps is sent. Measurement is turned off when your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, and you can turn it off at the bottom of every page.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when a feature materially changes data handling. The date at the top identifies the current version.

Contact and support

Questions about privacy or support can be sent to support@recordpicker.app. Support information is available at recordpicker.app/en-gb/support.
