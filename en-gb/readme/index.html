Features

Everything Record Picker does

Features marked PRO need Record Picker Pro; everything else is in Light. Up to 100 records, the free version also includes the advanced tools, except new Pro-only features.

Compare plans

Choose what to play

Random Pick

A weighted draw from your records: avoid recently played artists, favour your favourites or draw only from them.

Mood Pick

Describe a mood in a few words: a local suggestion, using an on-device Apple model when available.

Today’s Pick

A reliable music event (anniversary, reissue, news, optional nearby concert) linked to a record you own. When nothing reliable turns up, the app says so.

Listening JourneysPRO

An explained run of records, for a number of records or a listening time.

Listen Later

An ordered queue of records you already own.

Listening links

Open the album in Apple Music, Spotify, Deezer, Tidal, Qobuz, YouTube Music, Amazon Music or SoundCloud.

Catalogue

Collection and wishlist

Mosaic or list, physical formats (vinyl, CD, SACD, MiniDisc…), original and edition years, favourites, tags, notes, history and custom artwork.

Quick add

Barcode or cover photo on iPhone and iPad, scanning on Mac with a camera or your iPhone via Continuity, Discogs reference, manual entry with autocomplete.

Import, export, backup

Discogs-compatible CSV import, open CSV and JSON exports, complete versioned backups, verified non-destructive restore.

Metadata and artwork

MusicBrainz and Discogs for details; Cover Art Archive, Apple’s public catalogue and Deezer for artwork. Every source is shown and nothing changes without you.

Look after it

Data Quality

Missing covers, tracks, dates, labels, genres, formats, countries, barcodes or reviews; likely duplicates to merge or keep.

Classical music

On Mac and iPad: works, composers, conductors, ensembles, performers and editions linked together.

Critical reviews

Apple Music, Last.fm, Wikipedia, TheAudioDB, Genius and The Guardian; your hand-written text is protected.

Review keywords and batch enrichmentPRO

A musical vocabulary analysed on your device, and enrichment for many records at once.

Explore

Collection GraphPRO

The documented links between your records: musicians, labels, journeys.

Advanced statisticsPRO

What your collection holds and what it says.

Collection Stories

A timeline of acquisitions and listening, shareable as a PNG card with limited details.

Pressing PassportPRO

Runout, label, catalogue number, private notes and photos for each copy.

Ordered recordsPRO

Expected delivery date and tracking link.

Wishlist buying preferencesPRO

Budget, desired condition and priority, then guided set-up of Discogs alerts.
