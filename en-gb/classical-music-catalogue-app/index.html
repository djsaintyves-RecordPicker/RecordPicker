Classical music

An app to catalogue classical music

A classical collection needs more than an album title. Record Picker links works, composers, conductors, ensembles, performers and physical editions.

Download · App StoreMicrosoft Store

Free for up to 100 records · No subscription · No account

What Record Picker gives you

  • Describe composer, work and performers
  • Tell conductors, orchestras and editions apart
  • Organise box sets and multi-disc releases

How to start

  1. Import your collection or add your records.
  2. On Mac and iPad, let the classical analysis link works and performers from MusicBrainz, changing nothing when a match is ambiguous.
  3. Browse the collection by composer, conductor or ensemble.
