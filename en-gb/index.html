Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for up to 100 owned records. Beyond that, Light or Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. The App Store shows the price in pounds before you buy.

I use Discogs. Do I have to type everything again?

No. Export your collection or wantlist as CSV from Discogs and import it: a preview shows what will be added.

I bought Light. Is Pro cheaper for me?

No: Light and Pro are separate purchases. A Light purchase is not deducted from the Pro price.

Does it work with Spotify or Apple Music?

Record Picker opens the chosen album in your streaming service: Apple Music, Spotify, Deezer, Tidal, Qobuz and more.
