Today's Pick

Why this record today?

Record Picker 1.9 introduces Today's Pick: a timely, private reason to rediscover a record you already own.

  • Verified music news, significant anniversaries and optional nearby concerts are matched with your collection entirely on your device.
  • Every suggestion explains why it was chosen and cites its dated source. Wish list news remains separate from records you can play.
  • Optional private reminders, relevance feedback and Today's Pick on Apple Watch help keep suggestions useful. Your collection is never sent to the news service.

Record Picker

Choose what to play without losing control

Record Picker catalogues vinyl, CDs and favourite albums, then suggests the next record to play based on filters, favourites, exclusions and the mood of the moment.

Fair draw

Favor less-played records, filter by year, genre, format, speed, or favorites, then swipe the cover to draw or undo.

A well-kept collection

Barcode scan, MusicBrainz, Discogs fallback, custom covers, duplicates, exclusions, wishlist, and browsable history stay easy to manage.

iPhone landscape

Cover on the left, full metadata on the right, centered Pick button, and floating toolbar for frictionless choosing.

Faster favorites

A red star replaces the Favourite chip in the crate: one tap on iPhone or iPad is enough to mark or unmark.

Privacy

Record Picker has no user account, no advertising, no data selling and no Record Picker server storing the collection.
