Choose a record without going in circles

Start with one simple constraint: available time, format or genre. Filter the collection instead of browsing every sleeve.

Random Pick suggests a choice from that selection. Mood Pick starts from an intention or atmosphere; availability depends on your device and features. Record of the Day offers another starting point.

You can always try another suggestion. When one fits, listen and log it afterwards. The goal is to choose, not to accumulate suggestions.

Expected result: a record chosen for the moment without browsing the entire collection.
