Record Picker 1.9 · Today Pick

Record Picker 1.9 introduces Today Pick: a timely, private reason to rediscover a record you already own.

A timely reason to rediscover a record you already own.

  • Verified music news, significant anniversaries and optional nearby concerts are matched with your collection entirely on your device.
  • Every suggestion explains why it was chosen and cites its dated source. Wishlist news remains separate from records you can play.
  • Optional private reminders, relevance feedback and Today Pick on Apple Watch help keep suggestions useful. Your collection is never sent to the news service.
