Contact us

Email support@recordpicker.app and mention Physical Routine, the app version, iPhone model and iOS version. Describe the steps that lead to the issue. Hide health and personal information in screenshots. Technical support does not provide medical advice.

Questionnaire and equipment

Settings contains your questionnaire, three priorities, availability and equipment. The selected weight is per dumbbell and describes your equipment, not a mandatory load. Started sessions are preserved; future sessions may be recalculated.

Planning and guided sessions

Move or skip sessions in Planning. Later dates may adapt to preserve recovery. Guided rest periods can be extended. Stop if you experience pain or feel unwell, and seek professional assessment when needed.

Reminders

Enable them in Settings → Session reminders. After denying permission, use iOS Settings → Notifications → Physical Routine. Focus modes may delay delivery. Open the app regularly to refresh upcoming reminders.

Health and Apple Watch

Health access is optional and can be revoked. Keep the Watch paired and connected; if a remote confirmation fails, continue on iPhone. Check permissions on each device. A workout is exported to Health only with a confirmed duration.

Compatibility and availability

My Physical Routine is now available on the App Store. Build your routine at your own pace.

My data

In Settings → My data, control widget planning sharing or erase your local data. This does not delete workouts already saved in Health or older backups.
