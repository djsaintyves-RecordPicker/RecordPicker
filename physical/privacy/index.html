Privacy

How Physical uses your information.

EnglishFrançais

Current version

Physical is an adult strength-training app under development. It currently has no account, advertising SDK or analytics tool. Programs are calculated on your iPhone. The developer does not automatically receive your questionnaire, sessions, measurements, photos or Health data.

Local information

The questionnaire may include age, height, weight, goals, availability and health or surgery information. Answers, plans and progress are stored in app files. Photos and body measurements are optional. Physical requests that personal files be excluded from automatic backups; earlier backups may retain copies. Deleting the app removes its local data but may not remove backup copies. Individual body journal entries can be deleted in the app.

Apple Health

Access is optional and requires Apple permissions. The current version reads permitted steps and workouts and can save a completed session with a confirmed duration. Sleep and heart-rate importing are not yet implemented. Access can be changed in Health settings. Deleting Physical does not automatically delete workouts already saved in Health. Health data is not used for advertising targeting.

Watch, widget and reminders

The paired Watch receives session information and sends confirmations to the iPhone. The widget receives a planning summary. Optional local reminders contain no health measurements and can be disabled in Physical or iOS Settings. Preferences and tutorial history are stored on the device.

External services and support

Opening a web or App Store link connects you to the relevant service under its own privacy policy. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical information, including IP addresses, when you visit. These Physical pages contain no advertising, application cookies or analytics. If you email support, your address and message are used to answer and follow up on your request. Do not send medical records or Health data; technical support does not need them.

Future changes

This policy describes current functionality. Advertising, purchases or new Health categories will require updated information and, where needed, permissions before use.

Contact
