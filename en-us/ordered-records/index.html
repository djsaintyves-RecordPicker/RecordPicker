Ordered records: a preview of Record Picker Pro

Buying a record does not mean it has arrived. We are preparing an Ordered Records space between your wishlist and your Record Crate.

Wishlist→ Ordered records→ Record Crate

From wishlist to delivery

Mark a record as ordered from its menu, then mark it as received when it arrives. An accidental receipt can be moved back to orders.

The useful details

Seller, order date, shipping date — or “unknown” —, record price, shipping costs and a tracking link.

A specific edition

Add an order using a Discogs release reference or edition link, or enter its details manually.

On Mac and iPhone

Development includes a long-press action on iPhone and moving records between Collection sections on Mac. Dates and amounts follow the app language.

This Pro feature is being developed and verified on Mac and iPhone. It is not yet published on the App Store or Microsoft Store. The tracking link opens the carrier’s website; RP does not automatically check parcel status.
