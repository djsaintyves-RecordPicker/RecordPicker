iPhone · 3.1.1 · Record Picker Pro

Ordered records with Record Picker Pro on iPhone

Buying a record does not mean it has arrived. On iPhone, Record Picker Pro 3.1.1 includes an Ordered Records space between your wishlist and your Record Crate.

Wishlist→ Ordered records→ Record Crate

From wishlist to delivery

Mark a record as ordered from its menu, then mark it as received when it arrives. An accidental receipt can be moved back to orders.

The useful details

Seller, order date, shipping date — or “unknown” —, record price, shipping costs and a tracking link.

A specific edition

Add an order using a Discogs release reference or edition link, or enter its details manually.

On Mac and iPhone

On iPhone, use the record menu to manage an order. Moving records between Collection sections on Mac is still in preparation. Dates and amounts follow the app language.

This Pro feature is available on iPhone with Record Picker 3.1.1. The Mac version is still in preparation; this feature is not advertised as available on Windows. The tracking link opens the carrier’s website; RP does not automatically check parcel status.
