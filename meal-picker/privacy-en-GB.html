Who makes Meal Picker?

Meal Picker is developed by Yves Durand. For questions about this information or your data, email support@recordpicker.app.

Local library and Apple account

Meal Picker requires no Meal Picker account, displays no advertising and uses no audience-tracking tools. The developer does not receive your library and does not operate a server to collect your recipes or preferences.

Recipes, edits, notes, photos, translations, menus, shopping, favourites, cooking dates, history and preferences are saved in the app’s files. Language is a local setting. Ingredient exclusions and allergen declarations may reveal personal preferences; analysis runs on the device.

When your iCloud account is available, synchronisation uses your private CloudKit database at Apple to keep library data available on your devices. This is separate from sharing with other people. iCloud and system backups follow your Apple account’s settings and terms. The local library remains usable when iCloud is unavailable.

Seasonal region and optional location

You can select a country or region manually without using location.

Only after you choose to detect your region does Meal Picker request Apple’s location permission and make a one-time location request, followed by geocoding using Apple’s services. The app saves only the identified seasonal region. Coordinates are not stored in your library or sent to the developer. The app does not track your movements in the background. You can refuse or revoke location permission in system settings.

Images, paste and translation

The app receives images you explicitly choose in Photos or Files, or paste. The system picker does not grant Meal Picker general access to your photo library. A reduced JPEG copy is saved with the recipe without the original GPS metadata; the original remains unchanged.

Image searches open outside Meal Picker. To add an image, return to the app and use Photos, Files or paste. This public edition does not download images directly from a web provider.

Translation uses Apple Translation on the device. Apple may download language models. Original text and applied translations are saved in the library; synchronised data follows the iCloud behaviour described above.

Searches, sources and videos

In the public edition, metadata and image searches, sources and videos open in your browser or another app after your action. Meal Picker uses no embedded web view or remote player for these features and makes no direct API requests to DuckDuckGo, Google, Wikimedia Commons or Bring!

A search may include the title you chose. The service you open may receive that search, your IP address and the usual browser or app information. Its cookies and data retention follow its own settings and privacy policies. Opening a link does not automatically send your entire library.

Information you subsequently add to a recipe, with its sources and credits, stays in your library and follows the private sync described above.

Bring!, clipboard and sharing

Meal Picker lets you copy ingredients or share them through the system interface, then open Bring! externally using a generic link. You add or paste the items in Bring! The app does not automatically send a recipe URL, serving sizes or product details to the Bring! API or include them in its opening link.

Copying puts the chosen text on the clipboard. Sharing passes content to the destination you choose; information received there follows that destination’s rules. Meal Picker does not read your Bring! account or changes to its lists. Bring! applies its own privacy policy.

Sharing recipes and backing up your data

You can select one or more recipes to send in a .mealpicker file. It keeps ingredients, methods, photos, sources, credits, translations and links between preparations, including manually fixed translated titles. Linked preparations are included initially; you can disable this option. Personal notes are excluded initially and included only if you request them. Settings, favourites, menus, shopping, histories, cooking status and dates are excluded. Deleted recipes are not exported.

You choose where to save the file and who receives it. Photos and any notes you choose to include may contain personal information: review them before sharing. The recipient receives an independent copy they can keep or share again. Import adds new recipes; recipes recognised as already present are kept intact, including their edits, photos, notes and translations. This exchange does not link Apple accounts or set up synchronisation between people.

Export my data is a separate action for a complete personal backup. That file may contain recipes, photos, credits, notes, preferences, favourites, menus, shopping and history. It is not a recipe collection for sharing and cannot be loaded using Import recipes. Restoring a complete backup from a file is not offered in this version.

Reminders and Calendar

A planned cooking date is first saved in Meal Picker. Only an explicit Add or Update action creates or updates an external item containing the recipe title, date and link.

Reminders requests Apple’s system permission; the app looks up reminders it previously created and does not browse your other lists to build a profile. Calendar uses Apple’s confirmation interface, or an .ics file on Mac, without Meal Picker reading your calendars. These items may sync through the associated account. Changing or deleting the recipe in Meal Picker does not automatically change those external items.

Retention and your controls

You can edit saved information and remove a recipe from the library. In the current version, recipe deletion is reversible: the recipe remains in Deleted Recipes. This action therefore does not permanently erase its data.

Export my data creates a complete backup; Export recipes creates the shareable collection described above. Exported copies, Reminders or Calendar items and information sent to third-party services remain under your control at those destinations; deleting them in Meal Picker does not automatically erase them elsewhere.

Local data is retained while the app or its data remains on the device. iCloud copies and Apple backups follow account and system settings. You can manage Apple storage and permissions in system settings. The developer cannot access your private library or delete it remotely.

Support and updates

The support form prepares an email containing your message and the Meal Picker and system versions. You can edit it and choose whether to send it using your email app. Recipes, preferences and attachments are not automatically included. If you send the email, its contents and your reply address are received to handle your request; please avoid unnecessary sensitive attachments.

This information may change as app features evolve. The date above indicates the latest revision of this page.

Included, imported and shared content

The public edition includes only two original stuffing recipes by Yves and their translations. Recipes and illustrations from his private library are not bundled in the app.

You are responsible for content you import or share and the necessary permissions, including for third-party text and photos. Keeping a source or credit does not grant republication rights. Check the content and rights before sending a file.
