# Record Picker 3.0.1 - press kit

**Date:** 18 October 2026  
**Developer:** Yves Durand / Record Picker Studio  
**Press contact:** djsaintyves@mac.com

## Summary

Record Picker helps vinyl and CD collectors catalogue their library, find a record and decide what to play. It includes random picks, Mood Pick, Today’s Pick, search, filters and collection-exploration tools.

## Availability

Record Picker 3.0.1 is available on iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Windows.

- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/recordpicker/id6780422305
- Microsoft Store: https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9N2ZWRL4M3JC
- Website: https://recordpicker.app/

## Pricing

- Free: up to 100 records, inclusive.
- Light: €2.99 one-time purchase.
- Pro: €9.99 one-time purchase.

There is no subscription and no advertising.

## Key features

- Vinyl and CD cataloguing, collection import and search.
- Random picks, exclusions, favourites and listening history.
- Mood Pick and Today’s Pick to surface a record for the moment.
- Statistics, data-quality tools and visual collection exploration.
- Encrypted multi-device synchronisation through a folder chosen by the user.
